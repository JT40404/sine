SINE FORGE · EVERY LAUNCHPAD FEATURE IN ONE

Forge a coin. Pair it with anything. Tax the snipers.

Pair your coin with SOL, USDC or any Solana token. Then set your own fee scheme, a launch tax that starts high and decays to stop snipers and bots, creator revenue, team vesting and locked liquidity. Graduation to a Meteora pool is automatic. After launch, route creator fees into buyback & burn, added liquidity, or payouts to holders and partners. Your wallet signs everything. SINE never holds your keys or funds.

Token standard, supply, authority & vanity address
